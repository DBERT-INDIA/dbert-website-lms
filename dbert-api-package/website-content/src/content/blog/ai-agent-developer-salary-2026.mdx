### 1. Entry-Level Agent Developers (0–2 Years Experience)
* **Base Salary Range:** ₹7.5 LPA to ₹16.0 LPA (Top tier venture-backed: up to ₹22 LPA).
* **Role Requirements:** Writing deterministic tool integrations for agent workflows, testing prompts and system instructions, building retrieval ingestion scripts, and designing robust API validation wrappers.
* **Primary Differentiation:** Freshers entering through specialized technical ecosystems or practical incubators like the [AI Engineer Launchpad](/learners/launchpad) command a 40–60% compensation premium over peers possessing only generalist computer science degrees without proven LLM portfolio deployments.

### 2. Mid-Level LLM Systems Engineers (2–4 Years Experience)
* **Base Salary Range:** ₹16.0 LPA to ₹36.0 LPA.
* **Role Requirements:** Designing high-throughput Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, managing vector database infrastructure (Milvus, Qdrant) at multi-million document scale, executing parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA / QLoRA) on custom datasets, and debugging memory overheads during live inference.
* **Primary Differentiation:** Demonstrated competence in stabilizing non-deterministic system failures and architecting automated evaluation harness loops (LLM-as-a-judge frameworks) that prevent regression during baseline model upgrades.

### 3. Senior AI Architects & Research Leads (4+ Years Experience)
* **Base Salary Range:** ₹35.0 LPA to ₹85.0 LPA (Excluding cash bonuses and equity participation).
* **Role Requirements:** Directing hardware compute budget allocation, quantizing production models for low-latency edge deployment, architecting sovereign, compliant private hosting clusters, and spearheading mission-critical applied domain intelligence systems (such as financial or legal synthesis engines).
* **Primary Differentiation:** Engineers capable of operating across both bare-metal system performance Optimization and mathematical model evaluations represent the most constrained labor cohort in India's technology ecosystem.

## Equity vs. Cash Balance in Indian Startups

When evaluating technical job offers in India, engineers must scrutinize the equity composition. Early-stage AI startups frequently compensate for mid-band cash base salaries by granting high-upside stock option plans (ESOPs) or formal profit-sharing arrangements.

Understanding cap table dilution, strike prices, and acquisition vesting terms is vital for long-term career monetization. Developers frequently collaborate with early founders through non-dilutive framework engagements or specialized build-for-equity structures before transitioning to full-time Executive Architect roles. To maximize compensation trajectory within the Indian market in 2026, developers must transition rapidly from passive framework integration to deep systems architecture. Relying purely on transient external framework wrappers exposes your career to automation churn.
