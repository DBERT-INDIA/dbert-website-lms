§ 03 — HONEST DISCLOSURE: WHO THIS IS FOR (AND NOT FOR)

Incubation Eligibility & Selection Criteria

Who Incubation Is Built For:

  • Technical founders needing agile execution squads: You understand systems architecture but require immediate, reliable engineering muscle to ship frontend interfaces, data pipelines, and testing suites.
  • Domain experts with validated proprietary datasets: You possess industry-specific training workflows or distribution channels (e.g., healthcare, fintech, legal) but lack an internal MLOps team to operationalize local LLMs.
  • Pre-seed teams optimizing cash runway: You prefer conserving valuable financial reserves for customer acquisition by deploying a verified services-against-equity model (2–8%) for core product engineering.

Who Should Look Elsewhere:

  • Founders seeking instant cash-only VC investment: We are hands-on software developers and operational co-builders, not passive financial venture funds or speculative institutional bankers.
  • Projects without core AI/ML technical leverage: If your startup is a simple e-commerce storefront or basic agency wrap without algorithmic value differentiation, our specialized MLOps infrastructure is not a match.
  • Teams looking to outsource responsibility: We co-develop alongside dedicated founders. If you cannot attend weekly code reviews or architectural sprint alignment demos, our squads cannot operate effectively.
[STUDIO MANIFESTO // RADICAL CANDOR]

Why We Say No: 3 Candid Rejection Realities

We reject approximately 80% of applications. Because our senior engineers sit on your GitHub commits and take equity risk, we cannot take vanity projects. Here is why we usually say no:

1. Zero Algorithmic Moat

Thin OpenAI API wrappers with no proprietary domain heuristics, no private data pipelines, and no local fine-tuning cannot survive commodity competition. We only build ventures with architectural substance.

2. The Absentee Founder

We do not build for founders who want to check in once a month. Our squads operate on 2-week linear sprints. We require founders who join PR reviews, test edge cases, and talk to users daily.

3. Premature Vanity Scaling

Founders asking for a 20-person team before achieving proof-of-value. We believe in lean, hyper-competent 3–5 engineer squads that ship working production code in weeks, not bloated corporate overhead.
