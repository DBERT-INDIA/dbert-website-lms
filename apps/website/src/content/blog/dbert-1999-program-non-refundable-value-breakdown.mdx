Whenever a technical program charges an upfront fee and states **"₹1,999 is non-refundable,"** candidates are right to ask hard questions. Transparency matters more than marketing slogans—so here is the exact breakdown of what the ₹1,999 fee covers, why it is non-refundable, and how to decide if it fits your goals.

## 1. What "₹1,999 Non-Refundable" Actually Means

Under DBERT's [Refund & Program Policy](/refund), the **₹1,999 one-time incubation fee** (often structured as a **₹499 caution deposit + ₹1,500 balance** on [/enroll/1999](/enroll/1999)) is non-refundable once seat allocation and repository onboarding begin.

Why? Unlike pre-recorded video courses that cost zero marginal effort per student, DBERT provisions **human engineering bandwidth and active infrastructure** for every enrolled fellow:
- Private GitHub repository access and staging database schemas
- Assigned mentor hours for asynchronous and 1:1 pull request reviews
- LMS cohort tracking, milestone audits, and cryptographic credential issuance

Because these resources are committed the moment your cohort seat is locked, the fee cannot be refunded mid-sprint. That is **not** a hidden catch—it is the operating model that keeps the total cost at ₹1,999 instead of ₹25,000+ bootcamps.

## 2. The 6 Concrete Deliverables Included in the ₹1,999 Program

Evaluate the fee against what the program commits to deliver over the 2-month (8-week) cycle:

1. **Guaranteed Assignment to an Active Client Repository:** You are not stuck building a weather app on `localhost`. You work inside a live venture or client codebase.
2. **Individual Mentor Code Reviews:** Senior engineering staff review your pull requests, flag architectural regressions, and guide refactoring before code merges.
3. **Structured Two-Month Training:** A clear 8-week cadence—Weeks 1–2 onboarding & architecture, Weeks 3–6 active sprint PRs, Weeks 7–8 system hardening & evaluation.
4. **Live Production Sprint Assignments:** Real tickets covering APIs, RAG pipelines, security hardening, Playwright QA, OCR, or bot integrations.
5. **Project Placement with DBERT or a Hiring/Channel Partner:** Upon successful sprint completion, fellows are routed into internal DBERT portfolio squads (such as Alkame, Gayatri AI, or Aivara) or external hiring/channel partner pipelines, plus featured in the [Pre-Vetted Engineers Directory](/verify/directory).
6. **Offer Letter & Verifiable Credentials After Completion:** Official offer letter, completion certificate, and rubric dossier verifiable permanently at [dbert.in/verify](/verify).

## 3. Unpaid Incubation vs. Milestone Stipend Eligibility

Let's be unambiguous: **do not join the ₹1,999 program expecting an immediate monthly salary.** It is a project-based technical incubation designed to turn learners into production-ready engineers.

Where applicable on qualifying verified cohorts, fellows who pass **Gate 1 verification** and clear **strict GitHub PR + LinkedIn milestone audits** become eligible for **milestone-based ₹5,000 stipend releases** and priority placement introductions. Even without a stipend, the verifiable engineering portfolio and mentor-reviewed PR history routinely outperform months of unguided solo study.

## 4. How the ₹499 + ₹1,500 Balance Ledger Works

If you already paid the **₹499 caution deposit**, you do **not** pay ₹1,999 again:
- **Total Program Fee:** ₹1,999
- **Caution Deposit Credited:** -₹499
- **Net Remaining Balance Payable:** **₹1,500**

You can look up your profile instantly by Certificate ID or email on [dbert.in/enroll/1999](/enroll/1999), pay via UPI QR or 1-tap UPI app, and submit your 12-digit UTR for instant tracking.
