Whether you are a student completing your cohort onboarding or an engineering recruiter validating a candidate's resume, DBERT's verification and enrollment workflows are built to be transparent and self-service.

This guide answers the four most common **"How-To"** questions we receive every week.

## 1. How to Complete Your ₹1,999 Enrollment (Including ₹499 Deposit Credit)

If you are joining the 2-month technical incubation or completing your remaining balance after paying the ₹499 caution deposit:

1. **Open the Enrollment Portal:** Navigate to [dbert.in/enroll/1999](/enroll/1999).
2. **Autofill Your Profile:** Enter your Certificate ID or registered email in the lookup bar. The `/api/enroll/lookup` endpoint automatically populates your name, phone, college, and domain track.
3. **Pay the Exact Balance:**
   - If you already paid the **₹499 caution deposit**, your net payable balance is **₹1,500** (`₹1,999 − ₹499`).
   - Use the embedded UPI QR code or 1-tap UPI buttons (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).
4. **Submit Your 12-Digit UTR:** Enter your 12-digit UPI Transaction Reference (UTR) in the form. The portal assigns a unique submission ID (e.g., `DBERT-ENR-XXXXXX-XXXX`) and prevents duplicate submissions.

For a full breakdown of what the fee covers, read [Is the ₹1,999 DBERT Incubation Worth It?](/blog/dbert-1999-program-non-refundable-value-breakdown).

## 2. How to Verify a DBERT Certificate or Dossier

Any employer, university placement cell, or candidate can verify credentials in under 10 seconds:

1. Visit [dbert.in/verify](/verify).
2. Enter the **Certificate ID** (e.g., `CERT-DBERT-2026-DA-057`) or navigate directly to `https://dbert.in/verify/<CERT_ID>`.
3. Inspect the verification output:
   - **Verified Tier:** Displays the candidate's domain track, evaluation rubric, Turnitin originality status, and official 3-document credential packet.
   - **Provisional / Locked Tier:** Indicates the candidate is still in sprints or pending final verification/balance completion, with a direct link to `/enroll/1999`.

> **Domain Note:** `dbert.in` is the only active official platform (`dbert.online` permanently redirects to `dbert.in`). Always verify credentials on `https://dbert.in/verify`.

## 3. How to Get Ranked in the Pre-Vetted Engineers Directory

The [Pre-Vetted Engineers Directory](/verify/directory) is not a static alphabetical list—it ranks candidates dynamically by **verified status** and **evaluation score** (98, 97, 96...):

- **Tab 1 — Pre-Vetted Engineers (Verified · Ready to Hire):** Surfaced first to recruiters. Includes fellows who have completed their sprints, passed senior PR code reviews, and unlocked verified credentials.
- **Tab 2 — Incubation Fellows (Provisional · In Sprints):** Candidates actively progressing through their 8-week production sprints.

To climb to the top of Tab 1:
- Ship clean, well-tested pull requests during your sprint weeks.
- Address all mentor code review comments thoroughly.
- Keep your Turnitin originality score under 3.0% on documentation and case studies.

## 4. How Recruiters Hire Directly Without Markup

Recruiters visiting `/verify/directory` can filter by domain stack chips (Next.js, PostgreSQL, PyTorch, LangChain, Playwright, Docker) and score tiers (**90+ Top Tier Distinction**, **85+ High Merit**, **80+ Proficient**), inspect the candidate's **Proof Dossier**, and click **Connect on LMS** to engage candidates directly with zero recruiter commission.
