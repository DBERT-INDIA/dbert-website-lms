## Why Solo Self-Learning Stalls After Week 3

Spending two months learning independently sounds free on paper, but it carries three hidden failure modes that hiring managers spot immediately on resumes:

1. **Tutorial Echo Chambers:** Following a video to build a clone app does not teach you how to debug a failing CI/CD pipeline, resolve merge conflicts, or write a safe database migration.
2. **Zero External Feedback:** Without a senior engineer rejecting a poorly structured PR—explaining N+1 SQL queries, missing input validation, or broken auth middleware—bad habits compound silently.
3. **Unverifiable Claims:** Anyone can write "Built a SaaS app" on a resume. Without an independent evaluation rubric or a verifiable credential ID, recruiters treat solo projects as unvalidated claims.
