2. DBERT OPERATING FRAMEWORK

The DBERT Technical Council operates under government MSME registration (Udyam: UDYAM-UP-29-0109046), delivering advanced research, engineering incubation, and technical development infrastructure. Your engagement is directly with DBERT, and your training, assessments, mentor reviews, and milestone disbursements are administered through DBERT's proprietary learning management system (LMS).

3. EXPECTATIONS & PARTICIPATION

We seek candidates who treat this opportunity with professional diligence and integrity. You are not expected to arrive with mastery; rather, we expect consistent curiosity, responsiveness to mentor critique, active adherence to project deadlines, and a minimum of 10 logged hours weekly on the LMS platform.

4. FOUR-STAGE PROGRESSION PATHWAY
Stage Objective & Deliverables
1. Learn Orientation, architecture briefing, software fundamentals, and structured masterclasses.
2. Practise Curated assignments, code reviews, and milestone quizzes to build applied competency.
3. Contribute Execution of assigned sprint bounty tasks, bug fixes, and live repository pull requests.
4. Complete Final code review, comprehensive evaluation dossier, and cryptographic credential activation.
100% Council Sponsorship: DBERT Technical Council fully sponsors all training modules, cloud sandbox environments, developer tools, and evaluation infrastructure. You are never required to purchase any paid course or outside certification.
5. INTERNSHIP CAUTION DEPOSIT

Allocating dedicated mentors, computational sandboxes, and repository permissions commits immediate organizational resources. To secure candidate commitment, DBERT records a ₹499 Refundable Caution Deposit upon admission.

Refund & Credit Guarantee: The ₹499 deposit is a refundable commitment deposit only. It is 100% credited against your program ledger or fully refundable within 30 days of completing your 2-month tenure and required exit formalities. DBERT never charges candidates for the right to work.
6. THE ₹5,000 FIXED STIPEND & UP TO ₹18,000 PERFORMANCE INCENTIVE

Your internship compensation comprises a guaranteed fixed milestone stipend of ₹5,000 plus an additional performance-based variable incentive of up to ₹18,000, delivering a total earning potential of up to ₹23,000 across your 2-month tenure:

Milestone 1
10 Hrs / Wk
Maintain a minimum of 10 logged hours per week across the 2-month tenure on the LMS portal.
Milestone 2
2 Certifications
Successfully earn at least two (2) verified course certifications on the LMS platform.
Milestone 3
75 Bounty Tasks
Successfully complete and submit at least 75 bounty tasks reviewed and accepted by mentors.
Performance Incentive
Up to ₹18,000
Variable performance incentive earned upon technical milestones, code quality, and sprint reviews.
6.2 Variable Performance Incentive Terms: In addition to the fixed milestone stipend, interns are eligible to earn a performance-based variable incentive of up to ₹18,000. The evaluation criteria, sprint milestones, and rubric benchmarks governing this performance incentive are determined and awarded by the engineering evaluation committee based on sprint execution, repository contributions, and mentor assessments.

6.3 Payout Disbursement: Upon audit and verification of deliverables, the ₹5,000 fixed milestone stipend will be disbursed directly to your verified UPI ID via the LMS Wallet within 30 calendar days of tenure completion. Performance-based variable incentives will be disbursed following formal sprint evaluation approvals.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

During your tenure, you may have access to proprietary codebases, algorithmic architectures, datasets, and internal systems. You agree to hold all such information in strict confidence for a period of three (3) years post-tenure. All code, documentation, and deliverables produced during your internship shall be considered "work made for hire" and shall vest exclusively with DBERT Technical Council.

8. CODE OF CONDUCT & ZERO TOLERANCE

DBERT maintains a zero-tolerance policy regarding harassment, discrimination, plagiarism, unauthorized sharing of credentials, or malicious manipulation of platform data. Violations result in immediate termination of the internship and forfeiture of credentials.

9. CREDENTIALS & GOVERNING LAW

Upon successful tenure completion, DBERT issues an official cryptographic Certificate of Completion and Letter of Recommendation (LOR) verifiable at https://dbert.in/verify. This letter is governed by the laws of India, with exclusive jurisdiction in the courts of Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh.
